Exact Size

Exact window sizes. And the truth when they can't be.

Set the browser window or the viewport to the pixel, with zoom and window borders taken into account. Exact Size measures again after every change and tells you the size you actually got.

Free

Everything you need, at no cost

Viewport or window

Target the visible page area in CSS pixels, the same pixels your media queries see, or the whole window including its frame.

Measured, corrected, reported

After each change the size is measured and corrected up to three times. If the screen or the system sets a limit, you see the reason.

Presets your way

Common phone, tablet and desktop sizes to start with. Add, edit and reorder your own, synced across your Chrome browsers.

Keyboard shortcuts

Step to the next or previous preset, or jump to presets 1 to 4, without opening the popup.

Tab to a new window

Move the current tab into its own window at a preset size, ready for testing.

Side by side and 2 × 2

Arrange your windows on the current screen in one click.

Presets

Organised the way you work

Every preset has a size, a target and an optional position: keep, centered or fixed coordinates. Export and import them as JSON, with a clear message for every invalid entry.

Permissions

What it asks for, and why

Exact Size has no access to the websites you visit. It reads window and tab sizes through Chrome's window API, which needs no permission at all.
