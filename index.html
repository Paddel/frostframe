FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an account?

No. The extensions work without signing up. For a purchase you only enter an email address at checkout, so we can send you the activation key.

What data do the extensions collect?

None. Everything the extensions work with stays in your browser. The only network request is the activation of a purchase, which sends your key to Lemon Squeezy to check it. Details per extension are in the privacy policy.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once per extension and keep the unlocked features, including future updates of that extension.

Can I use my purchase on several computers?

Yes, one key activates up to 3 browsers. If you use Chrome sync, the unlock also follows you to your other Chrome browsers. Need more? Write to us.

Which browsers are supported?

The extensions are built and tested for Google Chrome on Windows, macOS and Linux. Other Chromium-based browsers that install from the Chrome Web Store often work too, but we don't test them.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Write to us first, we're happy to help. If it still isn't right for you, you get your money back within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. See the refund policy.

I lost my activation key.

It is in the order email from Lemon Squeezy. You can also look up your orders on Lemon Squeezy with your email address, or write to us.

Questions, bugs or ideas?

Write to us. Every message is read by the person who builds the extensions.
