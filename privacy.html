Cookie Desk

  • Cookies of websites: The extension reads, changes and deletes cookies of the websites you open it on, and only of those you allowed. This happens on your device. Cookie values, rules and snapshots are stored locally in Chrome and are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Activation of a purchase: When you activate a purchase, your activation key and a device label are sent to Lemon Squeezy and checked again at most once a week. The activation status is stored in Chrome's sync storage, so it can be available in your other Chrome browsers if you use Chrome sync.
  • No tracking: no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no account.
  • Deleting data: Removing the extension deletes all locally stored data. Snapshots and rules can also be deleted on the settings page.

Exact Size

  • No access to websites: The extension reads and changes the size and position of browser windows. It does not read the content of the websites you visit.
  • Presets and settings: Presets, groups and saved window arrangements are stored in Chrome's sync storage, so they are available in your other Chrome browsers if you use Chrome sync. The last applied preset, the active group and the size of your screen are stored locally.
  • Screenshot series: Screenshots are taken only when you start a series, and only of the visible part of the current tab. They are kept in your browser, deleted when you close the result page or start the next series, and never sent anywhere.
  • Activation of a purchase: as described for Cookie Desk above.
  • No tracking: no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no account.

Sure Fill

  • Forms on websites: The extension reads the form fields of a page and fills them with made-up values, only after you click its icon, press its shortcut or use its right-click menu, and on forms embedded from other sites only after you allowed that site. This happens on your device. What it reads and fills is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Made-up data only: Names, addresses and other values are generated on your device from built-in lists. It never uses your real personal data.
  • Rules, settings and profiles are stored in Chrome's sync storage, so they are available in your other Chrome browsers if you use Chrome sync. Test data from CSV files and the counter for templates are stored locally on the device. The last fill of each tab is kept in the browser's session storage until the tab is closed, so that it can be undone.
  • Activation of a purchase: as described for Cookie Desk above.
  • No tracking: no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no account.
  • Deleting data: Removing the extension deletes all data it stored. Rules, profiles and test data can also be deleted on the settings page.
