Sure Fill

Test data in every field. And the truth about the rest.

One click fills the forms on the current page with a made-up person whose name, e-mail, address and password fit together. Sure Fill says how many fields it filled and which it skipped, and why. It never submits a form.

Chrome Web Store: coming soon
Free

Everything you need, at no cost

Fits together

One made-up person per fill: name, user name and e-mail match, the repeat password is the same, postcode and city fit the country you choose.

Honest results

See how many fields were filled and which were skipped, with the reason: captcha, one-time code, read-only, a limit no value meets.

Limits respected

maxlength, minlength, min, max, step and common pattern expressions. If no valid value fits, the field is skipped, not filled wrongly.

Frames and frameworks

Embedded forms, shadow DOM, React, Vue and input masks. Forms from other sites are named, and filled after you allow them.

Unlimited rules, free

Fixed values, lists, templates such as test+{n}@example.com, or never fill a field. Patterns are compared literally.

Only made-up data

E-mail at reserved example domains, phone numbers from fiction ranges where they exist, card numbers only from payment providers' test numbers.

Rules

Your own values, where you need them

Rules match a field by name, ID, label, placeholder or CSS selector, top to bottom. Export and import them with your settings as JSON, with a clear message for every invalid entry.

Permissions

What it asks for, and why

No access to websites at installation. Sure Fill works on a page only after you click its icon, press the shortcut or use the right-click menu, and on embedded forms from other sites only after you allow them.
